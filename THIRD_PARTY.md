## Pokemon Yellow (Game Freak, Nintendo, Creatures)
`YellowData/` (maps, text, trainers, wild tables, species, moves, items, minimap and Town Map pictures, Pikachu's
faces) and `YellowMusic/` (songs and jingles rendered with new instruments) are extracted from the
[pret/pokeyellow](https://github.com/pret/pokeyellow) disassembly by `Tools/yellowdata` and `Tools/gbmusic`. The
underlying game data, text and compositions belong to their rights holders. Pokemon, Pikachu and all character and
place names are trademarks of Nintendo, Creatures Inc. and GAME FREAK inc. This is an unofficial, non-commercial fan
project with no affiliation or endorsement. To regenerate the folders yourself, run the tools; they clone pokeyellow
into `.cache/`.

## Generated art (Scenario)
The 3D models and painted textures under `Content/` were generated with [Scenario](https://www.scenario.com) on my
own account, from prompts describing the Game Boy characters and places.
